Incident Details

What Happened

A shooting incident was reported at Banga National High School in Banga, South Cotabato. Authorities responded to the incident and an investigation is ongoing to establish the complete circumstances surrounding the shooting.

Incident Timeline

Approximately 1:15–1:30 PM
Shooting incident reported inside Banga National High School.
Emergency Response
Authorities and emergency responders attended to the affected students and secured the area.
Investigation
Authorities began investigating the circumstances, firearm access, and other factors connected to the incident.

School & Community Response

Classes in the affected area were suspended as authorities and government agencies coordinated response and support for students, families, and school personnel.

Support for Affected Students

Government agencies and local authorities are coordinating psychological first aid and psychosocial support for individuals affected by the incident.
